How it is calculated
Manufacturer GST
taxable amount × rate ÷ 100 when the price excludes GST. taxable amount × rate ÷ (100 + rate) when the price already includes it.
Landed cost per unit
base + printing + shipping + operations + packaging + additional + manufacturer GST. Base and printing are counted once, whether entered together or apart.
Customer pays
selling price × (1 − discount). Sales GST is added on top when the price excludes GST.
Net revenue per unit
customer pays ÷ (1 + sales GST rate) when the price includes GST. Otherwise the discounted price itself.
Gross profit per unit
net revenue − landed cost
Selling expenses
gateway % × customer pays + fixed fee ÷ units per order + advertising + returns + other
Contribution profit
gross profit − selling expenses
Profit margin
contribution profit ÷ net revenue × 100
Markup
(net revenue − landed cost) ÷ landed cost × 100, using the price after discount and before sales GST
Break-even price
listed price at which contribution profit is zero. (landed cost + fixed fee per unit + advertising + returns + other) ÷ (net revenue share − gateway % × customer-pays share), then ÷ (1 − discount)
Units for target
target profit ÷ contribution profit, rounded up
Total profit
units sold × contribution profit. Units sold is the smaller of planned sales and stock.
Investment and ROI
investment = landed cost × quantity in stock. ROI = total profit ÷ investment × 100
Cash break-even units
investment ÷ (net revenue − selling expenses). Units to sell before the whole batch is paid back.
